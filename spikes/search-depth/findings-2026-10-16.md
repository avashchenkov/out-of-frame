# Spike findings, 2026-10-16

Script: [spike.py](spike.py), run on 2026-10-16 with the project's Tavily key.

## Do the pages that the agent's search misses show up with a wider or deeper search?

A label must be reachable: a Tavily search built from what the agent sees of the object finds
the label's page ([ground-truth labels](../../docs/eval-architecture.md#ground-truth-labels)).
The agent searches with 5 results at Tavily's basic depth, 1 credit a search. The review of
the step 10.2 labels, with the search of Codex and Grok, found pages that this search misses,
so their labels were left out or given another page. The script runs each of these searches
with 5 and 20 results at basic depth and 20 at advanced depth, which costs 2 credits a
search, twice each.

## Findings

- The pages are missed for three reasons:
  - **The cut to 5 results.** AMR's own page comes first among 20 results, yet not among 5:
    with a smaller limit, Tavily returns other pages, not only fewer. Leisten-Beyer's own page
    comes 15th or 18th at basic depth.
  - **Basic depth.** For Klärwerkstraße 1A, Sprenger's page comes 2nd to 5th at advanced depth
    and never at basic depth; for Klärwerkstraße 1, once 15th at basic and once 8th at
    advanced depth.
  - **Not found from the address at all.** LOXXESS at the NLND campus, the district's
    report on plan 8-81B, and the page that names the closed section of line 6107 are in no
    result. Codex and Grok found them with other words; an agent free to rephrase and to
    search more might too.
- Results change from one call to the next even with 20 results: AMR comes first in one run
  and is missing in the next.
- So a label left out for its page measures the limits the project set to save cost, not the
  agent: with 5 results at basic depth, these searches did not reach the pages. The pages of the first
  two kinds are within Tavily's reach at a higher cost; the third kind would need more
  searches with other words.
- Not planned, as the MVP is the stopping point: a run with 20 results or advanced depth
  would show how much recall that gains and what it costs per report.
